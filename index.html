Academic Profile

Dewa Putu Yudhi Ardiana

Doctoral Student · Information Science and Engineering

Ritsumeikan University · Osaka, Japan

I am a doctoral student in Information Science and Engineering at Ritsumeikan University, Japan. My research explores human-computer interaction and affective computing, with a focus on understanding learners’ frustration during programming and code-reading activities. I am interested in developing lightweight approaches to emotion-aware educational technologies.

Google Scholar ORCID ResearchGate

About

My work sits at the intersection of human-computer interaction, affective engineering, and educational technology. I investigate how signals such as facial action units can help infer learners’ affective states during programming-related learning tasks.
